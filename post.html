Comparing the Top AI Assistants in 2025:
A Hands-On Breakdown

Lorenzo Bartolo · 12 March 2025 · 8 min read

For two weeks I ran the same 40 prompts across four major AI assistants: GPT-4o, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Mistral Large 2. The prompts covered coding, research summarisation, creative writing, reasoning, and instruction following. Every output was scored blind by two independent reviewers on a 1–10 scale.

The headline: no single model dominates. Each has a distinct profile of strengths and weaknesses — and which one is "best" depends almost entirely on your use case.

Overall Scores at a Glance

The bar chart below shows each model's mean score across all 40 prompts. Claude edges ahead on the aggregate, but the margin is thin.

What I Actually Use Now

After this exercise my stack hasn't radically changed, but my allocation has shifted. I now lean on Claude for anything involving writing, nuanced instruction, or long context; GPT-4o for code generation inside an IDE; Gemini when I need something cross-referenced with current web content.

The best AI workflow isn't picking a winner — it's knowing which model to reach for and why.
