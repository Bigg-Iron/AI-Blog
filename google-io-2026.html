What to expect

Five reasons the AI world is watching

Google I/O 2026 isn't just a product refresh — it's the clearest signal yet of where the AI industry is heading. Here's what has the tech world buzzing.

AI Models

Gemini 4.0 — The next frontier

Google's most capable flagship model yet promises faster reasoning, richer multimodal understanding, and deep integration across every Google product. Early leaks hint at an Auto-Run agentic mode and persistent memory across sessions.

Hardware

Project Astra — AI glasses go real

Google's multimodal universal assistant is expected to make its consumer debut — or at least reveal a developer kit — for AI-powered smart glasses. See the world through Gemini's eyes, literally.

Android

Android 17 — The AI-native OS

Rumoured to be the first operating system built entirely around an AI core, Android 17 positions Gemini not as an app, but as the intelligence layer beneath the entire OS. This could reshape what a smartphone is.

⚡
Agentic AI

AI that just… does things for you

Forget prompting. Google is expected to unveil agentic AI capabilities where Gemini books your flights, manages your calendar, and handles multi-step tasks — autonomously. The era of AI assistants that assist passively is ending.

🎬
Multimodal

Gemini Omni — Unified everything

A leaked Gemini Omni model spotted inside Google's video interface just days before I/O suggests a unified model spanning text, images, audio, and video generation. One model to rule them all?

🛠
Developers

Google's end-to-end AI stack

Confirmed I/O sessions will cover Google's full developer toolkit: from robotics to real-time media generation. If you build with AI, this is your Super Bowl Sunday.

The big shift

From assistant to agent

The most important theme of I/O 2026 isn't any single product — it's a paradigm shift. AI is moving from a tool you talk to into a system that acts on your behalf.

Google's agentic push at this year's conference is expected to include:

  • Auto-Run mode: give Gemini a goal, walk away
  • Persistent memory that learns your preferences over time
  • Deep integration with Google Workspace for autonomous task completion
  • Android 17 as a wrapper for Gemini-native experiences
  • Cross-app agents that operate across your entire digital life
